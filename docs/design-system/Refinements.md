# Refinements

Where the app falls short of this system, and what to change. The brand book above states the target; this list is the gap, highest impact first.

1. **Two views: Mosaics and the editor.** **Now:** the app holds one mosaic. ☰ has _Open project or template…_, _Save project_, _Reset to defaults_ and _Forget image_. A project has no name apart from its card title, which defaults to "Mosaic Creator". **Change:** the Two views, Collection pages, Saving and Prompts conventions, the ProjectCard, the per-view ☰ and the name rules, as `.claude/plans/projects-library.md` lays out.

The first review (17 November 2026) found eleven gaps, all closed since: the Info bar, help text and its colour, one selected state, numbers and units, canvas placement, ColorSwatch, Toast and the zoom control, dark mode, retiring InfoList, and the phone app bar.

When you find a new gap, add it here as a numbered item: **Now** (what the app does, where), then **Change** (what the system asks for). Remove an item in the change that closes it.
